## Recommended directions

1. **Colorful / emoji-like:** IconPark's Multi-color family, or selectively MingCute.
2. **Soft Duotone:** Phosphor Duotone (currently used in sample output) or Solar, with attribution when required.
3. **Minimal and consistent:** Tabler, Iconoir or Lucide.
4. **High-contrast background badges:** Colorize a single family (Phosphor Bold is the initial example), preserving stroke, scale and clear space.
5. **Very broad inventory:** Combine *catalogues* for discovery, but keep each final Telegram pack stylistically coherent.

## Redistribution checklist

- Confirm the license for the **specific collection/file**, not just the vendor's homepage.
- Keep MIT/ISC/Apache notices and other applicable conditions; for CC BY 4.0 provide attribution, source link, license link and indicate changes.
- Do not assume that “free download”, a search preview, or a paid plan automatically grants repackaging or sublicensing rights. Check terms before importing assets.
- This project currently contains only Phosphor sample assets (plus links to other libraries); no Streamline/IconPark/Solar asset is included yet.

For source-library licenses, rely on the upstream license texts at the time of actual import, as terms or catalogues may change.
